♠SPADES NIGHTA card table for your crew
2–8 players · live rooms

How to play

  • Four players pair with the person opposite. Other table sizes play individually (cutthroat).
  • Each player bids how many tricks they expect to take. Bid zero for nil. Bids reveal when everyone has chosen.
  • Spades are trump. Follow the suit led when possible. Highest card in that suit wins, unless a spade is played; then the highest spade wins.
  • Make your bid: score 10 per bid trick, plus 1 for every extra trick (a bag). Miss it: lose 10 per bid trick. Every 10 bags costs 100 points.
  • Successful nil scores +100; a nil that takes any trick scores −100. Four-player scoring and bags are shared by the team.
  • After a complete hand, the first team or player at 500 points wins.
  • Cards are dealt evenly; any remainder is set aside unseen. The room host starts each hand.
